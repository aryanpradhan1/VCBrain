# FounderScore · 2:30 demo narration

Open the real app at `http://localhost:5174`. Keep the Pipeline, Network intelligence, Thesis Engine, one strong completed opportunity, and Apply page ready in separate tabs. The timing below totals exactly 2 minutes 30 seconds.

## 0:00–0:10 · The problem

**Show:** Pipeline overview. Do not click yet.

> The next exceptional founder is already leaving signals—in code, launches, papers, and a pitch deck—but venture capital still depends on warm introductions and weeks of manual diligence. FounderScore turns those fragmented signals into an evidence-backed decision within 24 hours.

## 0:10–0:29 · Find them first

**Click:** **Network intelligence** in the sidebar. Hover one company to isolate its full evidence path.

> We start with sourcing because that is the highest-leverage part of the VC Brain. This is not a filtered company list. It is a living graph connecting real founders, institutions, companies, public sources, and persistent Memory. It can surface a founder before they formally begin fundraising and trigger outreach into the same application funnel.

## 0:29–0:45 · Learn the channels

**Show:** Scroll to **Patterns in the verified cohort** and point to the sample-size label and underexplored-channel suggestion.

> The network layer also measures where opportunity is coming from. It compares accelerator links, research and PhD history, prior operator experience, and team structure. We label these patterns as descriptive—not causal—and expose the sample size. Concentration becomes a prompt to scan adjacent, underexplored communities, not a reason to reproduce one privileged network.

## 0:45–1:00 · One inbound and outbound funnel

**Click:** **Apply**. Briefly show the deck upload and optional exact public-profile fields, then return to Pipeline.

> Cold-start founders can apply without a prior network. A company name and deck are the minimum. Optional exact identity links improve confidence, while the document pipeline extracts slide text, images, claims, and citations. Outbound discoveries and inbound applications then converge into the same fast screening path.

## 1:00–1:15 · Fund-specific thesis

**Click:** **Thesis Engine**. Point across sectors, stage, geography, check, ownership, and risk.

> Every opportunity is evaluated through the investor’s configurable thesis: sector, stage, geography, check size, ownership target, and risk appetite. Exact matches use transparent if-else gates. AI is reserved for genuinely adjacent or ambiguous cases, so a deterministic decision never masquerades as model judgment.

## 1:15–1:35 · Three independent axes

**Open:** A strong completed opportunity. Point to Founder Score, then the three axis cards and their separate trends.

> Founder, Market, and Idea versus Market are three independent axes—never averaged. Each shows its own score or rating, trend, rationale, uncertainty, and exact citations. The persistent Founder Score lives separately in Memory, follows the person across applications, and grows sharper as execution evidence accumulates.

## 1:35–1:53 · Evidence and self-correction

**Show:** Processing trace and per-claim Trust Score. Click one deck-slide citation so the visual slide evidence opens, then close it.

> Every recommendation is auditable through the processing trace. The Trust Score is per claim, not one confidence badge for the company. The Validator cross-references traction, revenue, team, and market claims against bounded external evidence. A contradiction triggers a Memory update and recomputes the assessment instead of silently preserving a hallucination.

## 1:53–2:16 · Decision-ready memo

**Show:** Market chart, compact memo, and adversarial view. Click **Export investment brief** only if the PDF is already tested on this machine.

> The output is an investor-grade memo, ordered exactly like the challenge checklist: snapshot, hypotheses, SWOT, team, product, defensibility, market sizing, competition, traction, financials, cap table, diligence log, and exit perspective. It stays as brief as clarity allows. Missing information is explicitly flagged, never fabricated, while charts, calculation checks, sources, and the adversarial case make the decision easy to inspect.

## 2:16–2:30 · Close

**Show:** Recommendation and final decision controls.

> FounderScore moves from Sourcing, to Screening, to Diligence, to Decision. One investor gets the reach and analytical power of an organization, with uncertainty and evidence visible at every step. The system recommends a 100-thousand-dollar check within 24 hours; a human partner keeps final oversight.
