=== SLIDE 1: Problem & Product ===
Restaurants lose 8-12% of revenue to no-shows and last-minute cancellations, with no
reliable way to predict which reservations will fall through. NoShow AI ingests a
restaurant's reservation history plus public event and weather data to flag high-risk
bookings in real time, letting staff overbook safely and recapture lost revenue.

=== SLIDE 2: Market ===
The US restaurant reservation software market is $2.1B and growing 14% YoY. There are
roughly 660,000 full-service restaurants in the US; we estimate a $480M immediately
addressable segment among the ~180,000 restaurants already running digital reservation
systems.

=== SLIDE 3: Traction ===
Live in 34 restaurants across Austin and Chicago since our beta launch 5 months ago.
Restaurants using NoShow AI have recovered an average of $3,200/month in previously lost
covers. MRR is $18,400, up from $6,100 three months ago. Two regional restaurant groups
(12 additional locations) are in signed pilot agreements starting next quarter.
